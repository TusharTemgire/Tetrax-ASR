Fine-Tuning Tetrax ASR on IndicVoices

Trained and adapted on Marathi and Hindi conversational speech from Google Drive using LoRA on Google Colab's Free T4 GPU. The self-contained notebook is available at notebooks/Tetrax_ASR_Colab_FineTuning.ipynb.

Quick Colab Workflow Steps

  1. Open Google Colab (colab.research.google.com) and select Runtime > Change runtime type > T4 GPU.
  2. Upload notebooks/Tetrax_ASR_Colab_FineTuning.ipynb.
  3. Step 2 mounts your Google Drive; Step 3 loads Marathi & Hindi Parquet files directly from MyDrive/Tetrax-ASR/data/.
  4. Step 5 extracts 80-channel log-Mel spectrograms and tokenizes Devanagari labels.
  5. Step 7 attaches LoRA adapters (Rank=32, Alpha=64) to openai/whisper-medium.
  6. Step 8 fine-tunes using Seq2SeqTrainer with automatic checkpoint resume and high-throughput T4 batching.
  7. Step 9 packages the trained weights into Tetrax-ASR-Open-764M-A19M.zip.
